# Horizon Dental Studio

A fictional dental practice concept demonstrating the kind of complete service-business website and consultation-request system I can build.

## The Business Need

A service website should help people understand what a business offers, know what costs to expect and take the next step with confidence. Horizon explores those needs for a private dental practice through clear content, calm presentation and an approachable consultation journey.

## What I Built

- A responsive website that works across desktop, tablet and mobile layouts.
- Treatment pages that explain services and guide visitors toward a consultation.
- Indicative pricing with visible explanations of assessment-dependent fees.
- Practice/team information, clearly fictional patient stories and organized FAQs.
- A consultation-request form that saves validated example requests to a database.
- Optional email notifications to the practice after a request has been saved.
- Search and sharing metadata, keyboard support and clear form/recovery feedback.

The contact page includes a demonstration enquiry form that sends and stores nothing. Booking is the database-backed workflow; preferred dates and times are requests, not confirmed appointment slots.

## Why These Decisions Matter

Clear service information and visible starting prices help visitors decide whether to enquire. A consistent mobile experience makes that information usable on smaller screens. Repeated consultation links offer a clear next step without forcing a decision.

Saving the request before sending its notification protects the receipt when email is unavailable. A business can retain the request even if an optional notification fails. Shared content also makes names, fees and service links easier to maintain consistently.

## Delivery Evidence

The finished concept includes 12 public pages and a working consultation-request flow. A fictional live test verified database storage and delivery of a matching practice notification. The latest product QA recorded 41 passing automated tests and a successful production build.

Responsive, accessibility, search metadata and performance reviews are documented. These are technical delivery results; no increased enquiries, revenue or patient outcomes are claimed.

## What This Demonstrates

I can connect a considered business website to a real request-handling system, covering the interface, validation, database storage, notification integration and verification. A real service-business engagement would adapt the content, operations and privacy requirements to that business.
